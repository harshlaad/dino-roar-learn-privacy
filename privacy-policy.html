Dino : Roar & Learn

This Privacy Policy describes how Dino : Roar & Learn (the “App”), available on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store, handles information. The App is an educational product for children, used with guidance from a parent or guardian (“you”).

By downloading or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

The App is developed and published by the operator of Dino : Roar & Learn (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

Privacy / support contact: productionhouse450@gmail.com

2. Summary (store disclosures)

  • No account registration or password is required to use the App.
  • Learning progress and a child’s first name (or nickname) are stored on the device.
  • The App may use third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, push messaging, ads, and in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell children’s personal information.
  • We do not use the App to knowingly collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone audio for recording, or social media logins.

3. Information we process

3.1 Information stored on your device

The App stores the following locally (for example using on-device preferences storage):

  • Child’s name or nickname entered during onboarding
  • Age group and skill preference selected in onboarding
  • Lesson and quiz progress, streaks, and settings (such as reminders and sound)
  • Whether lessons have been unlocked via purchase or an optional rewarded/interstitial ad flow

This information stays on the device unless you clear App data, uninstall the App, or reset progress in the App (if available). It is not used to create an online user profile for the child.

3.2 Information processed by third-party services

To operate, improve, monetize, and support the App, we use the following categories of third-party services. These providers may process device and usage information as described in their own policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — Analytics (app usage events), Crashlytics (crash and diagnostic logs), and Cloud Messaging (notification delivery tokens). Typical data may include device identifiers, app instance identifiers, OS version, crash stack traces, and approximate usage metrics.
  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource — interstitial advertising used to unlock selected lessons. Ad networks may process advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple’s Identifier for Advertisers where available), device information, and interaction data necessary to serve and measure ads, subject to platform rules and parental controls.
  • RevenueCat and Apple / Google billing — to process and restore in-app purchases and subscriptions. Purchase receipts, product identifiers, and anonymized purchase status may be processed. Payment card details are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — store platforms process purchase and account information under their own terms when you buy or restore products.

3.3 Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the App schedules local notifications on the device. Notification permission is requested where required by the OS. Firebase Cloud Messaging may also obtain a device token to deliver messages. You can disable reminders in the App’s Grown-ups settings and/or in system settings.

3.4 Permissions

  • Notifications — for reminders and optional push messages
  • Internet / network — for ads, purchases, analytics, crash reports, and messaging

The App uses on-device text-to-speech for learning feedback. Speech is generated on the device and is not recorded or uploaded by us for voice profiling.

4. How we use information

  • Provide learning features, progress, streaks, and personalization on the device
  • Unlock content through purchases or ads
  • Send optional reminders
  • Measure stability and improve the App (analytics and crash reporting)
  • Comply with law and respond to support requests

5. Children’s privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K / store kids rules)

The App is intended for children and should be used with a parent or guardian. We design the experience so that core learning progress does not require creating an online account.

We do not knowingly:

  • Sell personal information of children

Parents/guardians can delete local App data by clearing App storage or uninstalling the App. For questions about children’s data, contact us at the email above.

Important for store listings: If you target Apple’s Kids Category or Google Play’s Designed for Families / Families Policy, you must configure ads, analytics, and SDKs to meet those program rules (for example, child-directed ad settings and limited data use).
